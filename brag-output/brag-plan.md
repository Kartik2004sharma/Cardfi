# Brag Plan: CardFi Yield Manager

## What is this app?
CardFi turns MetaMask Card activity into automated USDC yield management across multichain DeFi protocols.

## The angle
Your spending card should not leave idle capital behind. CardFi makes each swipe the trigger for a polished, visible yield loop.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
`EVERY SWIPE` resolves into `SMART YIELD.`

## Key moments (the middle)
- A MetaMask Card transaction routes into a Yield Manager.
- The real CardFi dashboard appears with its MetaMask Card integration and account metrics.
- Three promises land in sequence: smart rebalancing, multichain USDC, Circle wallet custody.

## Outro / punchline
`Spend normally. Let USDC do more.` followed by the CardFi lockup.

## User flow worth showing
Card spend trigger → smart contract rebalances USDC → CardFi dashboard reports card-linked yield status.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: restrained dark-finance product film
- Interpretation: confident typography, subtle green performance accent, soft cuts, no hypey claims.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 22 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #000000
- Accent: #22c55e
- Text: #fafafa
- Display font: Geist
- Body font: Geist
- Strongest visual element: CardFi's real dark dashboard screen.

## Share copy (draft)
CardFi turns MetaMask Card activity into smarter USDC yield management — with automated rebalancing, multichain access, and a dashboard that keeps the loop visible.
